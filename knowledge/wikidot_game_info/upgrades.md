## How to Read This Document

Each upgrade path section contains:

- **In-Game Description** — verbatim lore text from the wiki
- **General Information** — special damage type, when the path starts, max level, and key notes
- **Characteristics & Special Effects** — unique factors, scaling behavior, and special notes
- **Bonus Damage / Scaling Table** — stat bonus ratings (S/A/B/C/D/E/-) at each upgrade level for STR / DEX / MAG / FTH, plus any extra effects

## Meltstone (Reset)

### In-Game Description

> A shard of lava containing intense heat.
>
> This unique ore can melt all other ores and return an enhanced weapon to its original state.

### Characteristics & Special Effects

- **Used to return upgraded weapons to their original states**, so that they can be upgraded in different ways.
- When doing so, **any stones that were used to create the upgraded weapon are lost**.
- Example: If you find a Dark Heater Shield +2, you can use a Meltstone to return the shield to a standard Heater Shield +0.
- **Blacksmith Boldwin** in the Nexus can only return weapons with **basic upgrades** (Sharpstone and Hardstone) to +0.
- Weapons with prefixes or those forged from Demon's Souls require the skills of **Blacksmith Ed**.

### Material Availability

| Source | Location |
|--------|----------|
| Treasure | 2-1, near the first Fire Gecko |
| Drop | Rare drop from Fire Geckos in 2-1 |

## Crushing (Greystone)

### In-Game Description

> Grey ore that gives off a dull light. Enhances straight swords, axes, hammers, and so on.
>
> An ore used to make weapons whose sheer power can bludgeon foes. Weapons can be strengthened by Greystones up to a maximum of level 5.

### General Information

| Item | Value |
|------|-------|
| Special Damage Type | None |
| Upgrade path starts | +0 or +3 |
| Maximum Upgrade Level | +5 |
| Notes | Increases STR bonus, removes DEX bonus |
| Upgrade Target | Weapons (straight swords, axes, hammers, great swords, great axes, halberds, fists) |

### Characteristics & Special Effects

- The Crushing path is a **Strength-focused path**, drastically increasing the Strength scaling of the weapon.
- It **reduces base damage** and **completely removes Dexterity scaling**.
- The effects are **not considered elemental damage** — they are affected by stat scaling.
- A fully upgraded Crushing weapon is **among the highest damage weapons in the game** for Strength builds.
- Whether to choose the Crushing path depends on whether the build focuses on Strength.

>
> An ore used to make weapons that take skill to rend enemies. Weapons can be strengthened by Bladestones up to a maximum of level 5. Using bladestones to enhance a weapon can cause them to become brittle, so exercise caution.

| Item | Value |
|------|-------|
| Special Damage Type | None |

| Maximum Upgrade Level | +5 |
| Notes | Increases DEX bonus, lowers STR bonus |
| Upgrade Target | Weapons (daggers, curved swords, piercing swords, spears, katanas) & Spiked Shield |

### Characteristics & Special Effects

- The Sharp path is a **Dexterity-focused path**, greatly increasing DEX scaling.
- It **lowers the STR bonus**.
- Bladestone ore may **only be obtained from Skeletons in the Shrine of Storms** (world 4).
- The **Pure Bladestone** necessary to complete the upgrade path is only attainable as a **rare drop** from the Dual Katana Black Skeletons.
- **World Tendency glitch:** If the WT is Black but not quite yet Pure Black, the odds of the Pure Bladestone drop become astoundingly low — approximately **0.004%**. Ensure World Tendency is **completely Pure Black** before farming.

## Important Notes on Weight

> **Per the source page ([http://demonssouls.wikidot.com/upgrade](http://demonssouls.wikidot.com/upgrade) and its sub-pages):**
>
> The wikidot upgrade pages do **not** list per-weapon weight values. The "Confirmed Weapons" tables on each upgrade path page contain only:
> - Weapon name
> - Maximum damage at max upgrade level
> - Total stones needed for maximum upgrade
>
> **Weight data is not included** on any of the upgrade path pages. Weight information for individual weapons and shields would need to be sourced from the respective weapon pages (e.g., `http://demonssouls.wikidot.com/weapons`) rather than the upgrade page.
>
> Upgrade materials (stones/ores) also do not have weight values listed on the upgrade pages — they are treated as consumable items that occupy inventory slots rather than contributing to equip burden.
