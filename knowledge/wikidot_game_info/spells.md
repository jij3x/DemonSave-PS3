## Spell Types

There are **2 types of Spells** in the game:

- **Miracles** — associated with White Magic, such as healing Spells. They require a **Talisman** to use.
- **Magic** — associated with offensive or "Black Magic" Spells. They require a **Catalyst** to use.

There are eventually a total of **22 Magic Spells** and **12 Miracle Spells** available.

---

## How to Use Spells

To use a Spell you must:

1. **Learn** the magic Spell or Miracle you wish to cast.
2. **Remember** the Spell or Miracle by visiting the trainer.
3. Make sure you have **enough MP** for the Spell.
4. Equip either a **Catalyst** (for Magic) or **Talisman** (for Miracles) in any hand (in place of a weapon/shield), then use **[L1] / [R1]** to "attack" with that hand, which casts the currently selected Spell. Later in the game you may acquire the **Talisman of Beasts**, which allows casting both Magic and Miracles.

The currently selected Spell or Miracle is displayed at the top of the equipment menu at the lower left corner of your screen. Press **[Up]** on the D-Pad to scroll through all remembered Spells/Miracles.

### Why Casting Can Fail

If casting fails to work, there could be a few reasons:

- You do **not have a valid target** for the Spell (weapon enhancement Spells can only be used on **enchantable weapons**).
- Most personal **'buffs' will not stack** — only one can be active at a time (Anti-Magic Field, Second Chance, Water Veil, Protection, etc.).
- You are within range of an enemy player who has used **Anti-Magic Field**. When you are far enough away, you will be able to cast the Spell.

## How to Learn Spells & Trainers

### Stat Requirements

- You need to have a **10+ Magic stat** to learn Spells, and **10+ Intelligence stat** to have slot(s) to remember a Spell.
- You need to have a **10+ Faith stat** to learn and use Miracles.

When remembering Spells, you will see your known Spells on the left, with a small mark indicating the ones you have memorized. Click on them to toggle which are in memory. On the right you will see the number of slots you have available. Take note of the number of slots required to equip each Spell — some more powerful Spells take multiple slots.

### Initial Trainers

After defeating Phalanx, 2 new NPCs appear in the Nexus.

#### The Disciple of God

- Found in the area behind **Blacksmith Boldwin**.
- Teaches **basic Miracles** if your **Faith stat is at least 10**.
- Allows changing which Miracles are memorized.
- He **disappears once Saint Urbain is rescued**.
- He can also disappear *before* Saint Urbain is actually rescued if you select 'Talk' from his menu enough times (leaving you with nobody to help you learn/remember Miracles). You will know when he speaks of going to rescue Saint Urbain, and that it may be a while before you see him again. If this happens, remember Saint Urbain is easily rescued early on in **4-2**.

#### Freke's Apprentice

- Found in the area behind the **World 5 archstone**.
- Teaches **basic Spells** if your **Magic and Intelligence stat are both at least 10** (otherwise he calls you a "brute" and ignores you).
- Allows changing which Spells are memorized.
- He asks you to rescue **Sage Freke** from **3-1 Tower of Latria**.

### Advanced Trainers

Advanced trainers will only appear in the Nexus after you **rescue** them. They can teach you powerful new Spells if you offer them the correct **Demon's Souls**. You **can't leave the Demon's Soul with Stockpile Thomas**; you must have it in your inventory to learn the desired Spell.

| Trainer | Rescue Location | Teaches |
|---------|----------------|---------|
| **Sage Freke** | 3-1 Tower of Latria | Advanced Magic |
| **Yuria the Witch** | 1-3 Boletarian Palace | Advanced Magic |
| **Saint Urbain** | 4-2 Shrine of Storms | Advanced Miracles |

> Once Saint Urbain is rescued, the **Disciple of God leaves the Nexus**.

### Magic Slots (Intelligence)

An **Intelligence Stat of 40** provides for the maximum of **six Magic slots**. However, this can be increased to **seven slots** by equipping the **Ring of Magical Nature**.

## Player-Interest Notes

### Casting Failures & Limitations
- **Weapon enhancement Spells** (Enchant Weapon, Light Weapon, Cursed Weapon) can only be used on **enchantable weapons** — they will fail if the target weapon is not enchantable.
- Most personal **buffs do not stack** — only one can be active at a time (Anti-Magic Field, Second Chance, Water Veil, Protection, Cursed Weapon, Warding, etc.).
- **Anti-Magic Field** (a Miracle) suppresses all Magic casting within its area of effect. This affects both PvE and PvP — if an enemy player casts it, you cannot cast Spells until you move far enough away.

### Soul Farming Synergies
- **Soul Thirst** increases souls gained by **50%**. At 3 slots and 200 MP, it is expensive but powerful for farming runs.
- **Soulsucker** instantly kills any non-boss Demon NPC and awards **double souls**. Requires the Maiden in Black's Demon's Soul (End Game) and 3 slots.
- **Evacuate** is described on the page as **"incredibly useful for farming"** — it transports the caster back to the Nexus while retaining all collected souls, avoiding the risk of losing them on death.

### Survival & Utility Standouts
- **Second Chance** revives the caster with **50% HP upon death** — a premier survival Miracle (2 slots, 100 MP).
- **Warding** greatly reduces physical damage taken (2 slots, 50 MP).
- **Water Veil** reduces Fire damage taken and works against all types of Fire (1 slot, 20 MP).
- **Protection** slightly reduces physical damage taken (1 slot, 20 MP).
- **Cure** provides complete status recovery — cures Poison, Plague, **and** Bleeding in one cast (1 slot, 30 MP).

### Offensive Standouts
- **Firestorm** is a large AoE fire Spell — one of the most damaging Spells in the game (3 slots, 100 MP).
- **God's Wrath** is the Miracle equivalent — an AoE sphere of damage around the caster (2 slots, 100 MP).
- **Soul Ray** penetrates multiple enemies and is more powerful than Soul Arrow (1 slot, 15 MP).
- **Homing Soul Arrow** creates orbs that auto-track the nearest enemy (2 slots, 40 MP).
- **Ignite** offers fast, high direct fire damage at point-blank range (2 slots, 18 MP).

### Stealth & Deception
- **Cloak** makes it harder for enemies to see the caster (1 slot, 10 MP).
- **Hidden Soul** makes it harder for **Black Phantoms** to see the caster — useful in online PvP (1 slot, 30 MP).
- **Demon's Prank** creates a noise in the distance to distract enemies (1 slot, 4 MP).

### Damage Over Time
- **Poison Cloud** infects enemies with poison (1 slot, 15 MP).
- **Death Cloud** infects enemies with plague — more severe than poison (2 slots, 30 MP).
- **Acid Cloud** reduces enemy equipment durability — can degrade enemy weapons/armor (1 slot, 30 MP).

### Weapon Enchantment Tiers
- **Enchant Weapon** — adds *low* Magic damage; scales with Magic stat (1 slot, 20 MP).
- **Light Weapon** — adds *high* Magic damage; scales with Magic stat (2 slots, 50 MP).
- **Cursed Weapon** — boosts weapon damage by **50%** but gradually lowers HP (3 slots, 100 MP).

### Healing Tiers (Miracles)
- **Heal** — caster recovers *some* HP; scales with Faith (1 slot, 30 MP).
- **Recovery** — caster recovers *a lot* of HP; scales with Faith; more powerful than Heal (2 slots, 60 MP).
- **Relief** (Magic) — *fully* recovers a touched ally (1 slot, 10 MP).
- **Regeneration** — gradually regenerates HP over time (1 slot, 40 MP).

### Online / Co-op Specific
- **Resurrection** — nearby **Blue Phantoms** regain their body and return to their world (1 slot, 50 MP).
- **Banish** — returns nearby invading **Black Phantoms** to their own world (2 slots, 50 MP).
- **Anti-Magic Field** — shuts down enemy Magic casting in PvP (2 slots, 10 MP).

### Talisman of Beasts — Dual Casting
The **Talisman of Beasts** is a special item that functions as both a Catalyst and a Talisman, allowing a single character to cast **both Magic and Miracles** without swapping equipment. It is obtained either as a reward from the Mephistopheles quest (Pure Black Character Tendency) or as a drop from Black Phantom Scirvir in 2-2 (Pure Black World Tendency).
