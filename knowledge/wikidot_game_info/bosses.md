## What are Demon's Souls?

Demon's Souls refer to the souls of named bosses that you receive when they are defeated. When you defeat each boss, it would be in your best interest to **not consume its soul** if it is your first time playing through the game. Demon's Souls can be used by [Blacksmith Ed](http://demonssouls.wikidot.com/blacksmith-ed) to make unique weapons or by [Sage Freke](http://demonssouls.wikidot.com/freke), [Yuria, The Witch](http://demonssouls.wikidot.com/yuria), or [Saint Urbain](http://demonssouls.wikidot.com/urbain) to teach you [Magic spells and Miracles](http://demonssouls.wikidot.com/spells). [Blacksmith Ed](http://demonssouls.wikidot.com/blacksmith-ed) must also be given the [Red Hot Demon's Soul](http://demonssouls.wikidot.com/ds-red-hot) before he can upgrade weapons with other Demon's Souls.

#### Phalanx

| Stat | Value |
|------|-------|
| **Stage** | 1-1, Boletarian Palace |
| **Demon's Soul** | Lead Demon's Soul |
| **Health** | 1,150 |
| **Souls (reward)** | 1,270 |
| **Optional?** | No |
| **Normal Defense** | 112 |
| **Slash Defense** | 112 |
| **Blunt Defense** | 140 |
| **Pierce Defense** | 112 |
| **Magic Defense** | 98 |
| **Fire Defense** | 70 |
| **Poison Resistance** | Immune |
| **Plague Resistance** | Immune |
| **Bleed Resistance** | Average |

**Weaknesses:** Phalanx is particularly weak to fire-based attacks.

**Attack / Defence Patterns:**
Phalanx in itself is a harmless blob of darkness, having no real form of attack. It relies on its coating of Hoplites to attack you with spear throws and stabs (at close range). However, it can move, albeit slowly, to back the player into a corner, and release its Hoplites voluntarily in an attempted flanking maneuver. Phalanx has exactly 35 Hoplites upon spawning, but can regenerate them when they die, as well as create more to exceed the original count. As more Hoplites are slain, the remaining creatures will slowly move to cover the side of the Phalanx that faces the player, leaving the other side exposed.

**Regeneration Note:**
The Hoplites will respawn, after a period of around 2 minutes or so per small group of Hoplite, until the last one is slain. They will also regenerate their health, if they re-attach themselves to the Phalanx, after being hurt. The Phalanx will also regenerate its health, if not attacked for a period of time, until the last Hoplite is slain.

**Strategies:**

- **Melee (Strong Offense):** Two-hand your weapon, coat it with Turpentine, and attack Phalanx directly. Do not use lock-on. Retreat when stamina gets low. Works best for Knight, Priest, Soldier, Temple Knight, and Hunter classes.
- **Fire Strategy:** Use Firebombs to kill Hoplites (you can find 11 in 1-1 or buy from Dregling Merchant for 500 souls each). Apply Turpentine to weapon, sprint to the back of Phalanx, and attack the uncovered side.
- **Fire-less Strategy:** Run around the perimeter dodging spears. Kill Hoplites that break away from the back. When a hole forms, hack away at Phalanx.
- **Magic:** Hoplites can be taken out with Flame Toss or Soul Arrow. Keep moving sideways. Buy Fresh Spice from Blacksmith Boldwin before entering.
- **New Game+ (Large Weapons):** Dragon Bone Smasher, Great Sword, Northern Regalia, Demonbrandt, or Soulbrandt make short work of Hoplites' shields. Use Turpentine on enchantable weapons.
- **New Game+ (Blind):** The weapon Blind ignores Hoplites' shields completely. Switch to a stronger weapon after all Hoplites are dead.
- **New Game+ (Large Sword of Moonlight):** Ignores shields of Hoplites, making it easy to eliminate all minions.
- **New Game+ (Firestorm):** Hide behind a pillar and cast Firestorm once or twice to wipe out everything.
- **New Game+ (Death Cloud):** Drop a cloud directly on Phalanx and all Hoplites, then run around for a few minutes. The Plague damage will clear out all Hoplites. *(Note: using Death Cloud on Phalanx in Online mode may cause network strain, dropping Blue Phantoms.)*
- **New Game+ (Soul Ray):** One shot can hit 4–7 Hoplites and Phalanx itself. Circle around and fire when they're lined up.

**Victory:** After beating Phalanx, return to the Nexus and talk to the Maiden in Black. This initiates a scripted event allowing you to speak to the Monumental. After the event you can spend souls on upgrading your stats.

**Note for New Players:** Although it is possible to use Phalanx's Lead Demon's Soul to increase your souls, Demon's Souls can be used later to craft unique weapons or learn unique spells. It is not recommended to consume them until you know your options.

| **Magic Defense** | 62 |
| **Fire Defense** | 109 |
| **Poison Resistance** | Weak |
| **Plague Resistance** | Weak |
| **Bleed Resistance** | Immune |

**Weaknesses:** His head. His heels.

**General Notes:**
First sprint past the Tower Knight up the stairs on the left or right, and continue all the way around and kill all the crossbowmen on both sides of the courtyard, then pick a method of attack. The easiest way to get behind him is to run through his legs. If his shield starts to glow and he lifts it horizontally, just keep running through — he lunges forward with this attack. Sometimes while running through his legs you can get pinned between his leg and shield; don't panic, keep running. After knocking him down and hitting his head, he does a quick vertical shield drop upon returning to his feet — give him space.

**Strategies:**

- **Melee Strategy:** Sprint to get behind him and target his ankles. He will fall over after enough damage, allowing you to attack his head for massive damage.
- **Alternate Melee (Staircase Shuffle):** A safe but slow method — bait him to the bottom of the stairs, sprint down when he throws his lance, slash his exposed foot, then run back up. In NG+ having a Dragon Sword +3 or Magic weapon like Large Sword of Moonlight makes this preferable.
- **Ranged (Bow/Magic):** Stay on the walls, attack from the archway above the entrance. Use stairs and railing for protection. Move behind the railing when he uses his ranged spear attack, step out and fire when he lowers his shield.
- **Enchanting:** Enchant your weapon for the largest damage amounts.
- **Poison Cloud / Death Cloud:** Tower Knight is weak to both. Death Cloud will paralyze him, allowing you to walk up and swing away.
- **Fire Spray:** After killing archers, get behind Tower Knight, lock-on to his head, and hold R1 while circling to avoid all attacks.
- **Soul Arrow:** Tower Knight is very weak to Soul Arrow. Kill all archers first, then strafe and dodge his shield bash, firing Soul Arrow when exposed.

| **Magic Defense** | 148 |

| **Plague Resistance** | Average |
| **Bleed Resistance** | Immune |

**Strategies:**

- **General:** Penetrator has a large sword and uses sweeping attacks. The pillars offer no protection — his attacks destroy them. His sword attacks penetrate, dealing partial damage even when blocking with 100% shields. His attacks are reasonably slow. Avoid the "penetrating" stabbing attack (sword glows like Tower Knight's ranged attack). Roll through his attacks to avoid damage.
- **Ranged:** Walk around the room firing arrows or Soul Arrow (use Homing Soul Arrow; Firestorm also does hefty damage), rolling through swipes.
- **Melee:** Keep moving and keep your shield up. Stay out of range of sweeping attacks. **Key:** Only attack him right after his stabbing attack — if you attack after other attacks, he may switch to the fatal stabbing attack.
- **Spear/Shield:** Bait his forward lunging sweeping attack by staying just out of range. A Winged Spear's R2 attack (lunge) can hit after he sweeps.
- **Biorr:** If you rescued Biorr, he will assist, drawing Penetrator's attention. In NG+, Biorr can only take a handful of hits. Use this to cast Homing Soul Arrow and/or Firestorm. Do not cast AoE spells on the boss when Biorr is engaging him — he will turn hostile.
- **Fire Spray:** Lock on, hold R1, walk backwards in a big circle around the room.
- **Firestorm:** Dodge his opening swing, end up behind him, cast Firestorm. Repeat. Usually two casts are enough.
- **Soul Ray:** Use Soul Ray — one of the fastest projectiles in the game. Works with Magic stat below 20 but takes more time. Wear light armor to dodge.

#### Old King Allant (False King)

| Stat | Value |
|------|-------|
| **Stage** | 1-4, Penetrator Archstone |
| **Demon's Soul** | False King's Demon's Soul |
| **Health** | 5,967 |
| **Souls (reward)** | 75,500 |
| **Optional?** | No |
| **Voiced by** | Peter Marinker |
| **Normal Defense** | 207 |
| **Slash Defense** | 207 |
| **Blunt Defense** | 207 |
| **Pierce Defense** | 207 |
| **Magic Defense** | 217 |
| **Fire Defense** | 198 |
| **Poison Resistance** | Strong |
| **Plague Resistance** | Immune |
| **Bleed Resistance** | Strong |

**Weaknesses:** Old King Allant is highly resistant to magic and partially resistant to fire. He is difficult to knock down or stunlock unlike most humanoid-esque enemies.

**Strategies:**

- **Melee:** The Eternal Warrior's Ring helps with stamina. Purely physical weapons with range (Spears) work best. He has a unique **Soulsucker** attack at melee range which steals one Soul Level — don't stay too close. Constantly back away with shield raised. When he points his sword down and glows, he's about to do an area explosion — run up and attack to interrupt, then roll backwards. When he raises his sword at an angle, he's about to rush you — roll aside.
- **Exploiting Ranged Attack:** He has only two long range attacks (rush and explosion), both easily avoided. His shockwaves can be blocked by a decent shield. Keeping your distance makes him simple and eliminates risk of losing soul levels.
- **Ranged (Bow):** White Bow or Sticky Compound Long Bow with Heavy/White Arrows. Stay at a distance and hit him while he walks, charges, or attempts his glowing explosion. Never stay within Soulsucker range.
- **Super Cheap Ranged:** Wear the Thief's Ring and don't move after entering the fog. Manually aim at his head — if his life bar isn't showing, he won't attack. Use Hyper Mode (Morion Blade and/or Clever Rat's Ring with HP < 30%) for added attack power. ~150 arrows needed.
- **Magic (AoE Exploit):** Equip Soul Ray and Warding. Cast Warding immediately, keep him at medium distance, shoot Soul Ray when he charges his AoE attack. Keep recasting Warding (40-second duration).
- **Firestorm:** Cast Warding, rush the stairs, turn into a corner. Wait for him to charge, then cast Firestorm. Two casts kill him in Normal Game.
- **Poison Cloud (Super Cheap):** Wear Thief's Ring, cast Poison Cloud from the top of the stairs. Wait for it to wear off, recast. Repeat six times. Requires patience.

**Soulsucker Note:** If Old King Allant steals a soul level and you die during the fight, those souls are deposited with your bloodstain. If you beat him, the stolen souls are NOT returned.

**Attack Pattern:**
The Red Dragon in 1-2 makes 'bombing runs' along the length of the bridge, breathing fire which typically spells instant death. Wait till it passes, then sprint to safety.

**Strategies (1-2, Phalanx Archstone):**

- **Ranged (First Guard Tower):** Sprint past the first section. From the guard tower, use a Bow, Crossbow, or Magic. Anticipate timing and fire where the dragon will be. Bring at least 150 arrows. For Crossbow/spells, use quick lock-on/release to preset free-fire spot.
- **Alternate Position (Soul Arrow):** Easier to hit from the bottom of the first tower. Shoot right when the dragon dips its head to breathe fire.
- **Second Guard Tower:** Position yourself at the foot of the left board of the wagon. Center reticule above the brick sticking out of the wall. Fire the moment you hear the roar after the wing beat.
- **Royalty Ranged Magic:** With Magic 13, you deal 19 damage per hit (71 hits needed). Fragrant Ring provides MP regeneration.
- **God's Wrath:** Stand at the top of the tower. Cast right after the dragon roars. Small window — needs positioning practice.
- **Poison Cloud:** Cast when the dragon flies low on the first causeway. Four poison clouds over ~10 minutes kills it without arrows. Death Cloud does not work.
- **Melee (Long Weapon):** Use a long weapon at the start of the bridge. Swing at his tail when he flies by (don't auto-target).
- **Meat Cleaver "Chicken":** Enchant Meat Cleaver, play chicken with the dragon using 2-handed heavy attack. Take damage but don't get knocked down.
- **Firestorm/Second Chance:** Cast Second Chance, stand in front of charred bodies, cast Firestorm as he comes to breathe fire.

**1-1 Note:** The first encounter in 1-1 is an environmental hazard — it cannot be killed there. It leaves when health reaches ~10%.

**Weaknesses:** The Armor Spider is well-protected against fire, especially Firestorm. She takes significantly more damage from magic. Use Sticky White Stuff for melee, or a crescent/moon weapon.

**Attack Patterns:**

- **Melee:** Cross-leg sweep (1–3 sweeps), then raises body and smashes down with both front legs. Smash attacks followed by drawing up flames for a devastating inferno covering up to half the tunnel.

- **Ranged (Hyper Mode):** In NG+, Clever Rat's Ring + Morion Blade + White Bow + Heavy Arrows with 30 STR/DEX does a few hundred damage per arrow. Stand to either side, fire, dodge retaliation, repeat.

#### Flamelurker

| Stat | Value |
|------|-------|
| **Stage** | 2-2, Armor Spider Archstone |
| **Demon's Soul** | Red Hot Demon's Soul |
| **Health** | 3,720 |
| **Souls (reward)** | 22,500 |
| **Optional?** | No |
| **Normal Defense** | 160 |
| **Slash Defense** | 200 |
| **Blunt Defense** | 160 |
| **Pierce Defense** | 200 |
| **Magic Defense** | 23 |
| **Fire Defense** | 857 |
| **Poison Resistance** | Strong |
| **Plague Resistance** | Strong |
| **Bleed Resistance** | Strong |

**Description:**
Below the labyrinthine tunnels dug up by the Miners, within the very heart of Stonefang, a temple built by the burrowers to worship the bones of dragons is found. Should any incautious explorer get too close to its gates, they'll find themselves face to face with the fiery demon guarding it. Offering his infernal spirit to Blacksmith Ed enables Ed to enchant weapons with more Stones and create new arsenals from Demon's Souls.

**Attack Patterns:**
- **Fire Slam:** Smashes left hand on the ground creating an explosion. Can also cast a larger explosion by growling, raising both hands, then slamming.
- **Retreat:** After an attack, jumps away, damaging the player on contact.
- **Swipe:** Swipes either right or left hand. Can be followed by a second swipe.
- **Pounce:** Growls, crouches, then jumps at the player.

**General Tips:**
High mobility is key. Light armor with decent fire defense (Wizard's Clothes) is recommended. Characters with high Endurance can tank more (Endurance raises Fire Defense). Dodging backward twice avoids explosions. The Pounce is the safest attack to punish — Flamelurker takes a while to recover from it. As health depletes, he becomes more aggressive, closing distance with Pounce and chaining Fire Slams. His body shines brighter and attacks deal more damage.

**Recommended Equipment:**
- **Weapons:** Scimitar (fast, effective buffed with Sticky White Stuff/Light Weapon), Purple Flame Shield (90% fire block), Kris Blade (passive spell damage buff), Crescent Falchion +1 (MP regen + magic damage), Compound Long Bow (best range/damage), Blessed Mace +1 (HP regen + magic damage).
- **Spells:** Water Veil (fire defense), Enchant Weapon (magic damage), Soul Arrow (cheap, fast, safe range).
- **Armors:** Silver Coronet (MP boost), Wizard's Clothes (light, fire defense).
- **Rings:** Ring of Flame Resistance, Ring of Magical Sharpness, Clever Rat's Ring, Regenerator's Ring, Fragrant Ring.
- **Items:** Fresh Spice, Sticky White Stuff, Holy Arrows (pure magic damage).

**Strategies:**

- **Warrior:** Bring Regenerator's Ring + Ring of Flame Resistance (+ Water Veil) and Sticky White Stuff. Non-fire weapons equally effective. Daggers, Curved Swords, Poles (Halberd, Mirdan Hammer), and Katana are good choices. Heavy weapons (Great Axe/Club/Sword) are risky. Compound Long Bow with Holy Arrows for ranged.
- **Magician:** Extremely effective due to his low Magic Defense. Soul Arrow and Soul Ray (fast, long range, good tracking). Homing Soul Arrow can deplete a large portion of health. Use Kris Blade for passive buff. Manage MP with Fresh Spice/Old Spice.
- **Cleric:** Blessed Mace +1 (blunt + magic damage + HP regen), Large Sword of Moonlight (faith-scaling, pure magic). Adjudicator's Shield for HP regen. Regeneration miracle + Regenerator's Ring + Ring of Flame Resistance.
- **Turtle Tank:** High Endurance + Strength with Purple Flame Shield/Tower Shield/Brushwood Shield + Winged Spear buffed with Sticky White Stuff. Add Water Veil + Ring of Flame Resistance.
- **Hypermode:** Enter with <30% HP, Morion Blade + Clever Rat's Ring for 140% attack boost. Best for Bows/spells (especially Homing Soul Arrow). Use Second Chance. Magic Sword "Makoto" to drain HP.
- **Fireproof:** Stack Water Veil + Ring of Flame Resistance + high Endurance for near fire immunity. Note: melee attacks still deal physical damage.
- **Glitch Method (Long Range):** Exploit poor pathfinding. Run to the right behind the gigantic ribcage, draw aggro with an arrow. When Flamelurker enters the ribcage, run behind the spinal cord on the other side. He gets stuck. Deplete health with arrows/magic. *(PS3 only.)*
- **Glitch Method (Melee):** Equip Thief's Ring, walk right hugging the wall to get behind him undetected. Get behind debris on the staircase, remove ring, draw aggro with ranged attack. He gets stuck. Use only melee (ranged prompts sidestep). Don't get greedy — lower health = more damage/chaining. *(PS3 only.)*

#### Dragon God

| Stat | Value |
|------|-------|
| **Stage** | 2-3, Flamelurker Archstone |
| **Demon's Soul** | Dragon Demon's Soul |
| **Health** | 4,340 |
| **Souls (reward)** | 26,800 |
| **Optional?** | No (Archdemon) |
| **Normal Defense** | 206 |
| **Slash Defense** | 206 |
| **Blunt Defense** | 206 |
| **Pierce Defense** | 206 |
| **Magic Defense** | 206 |
| **Fire Defense** | 273 |
| **Poison Resistance** | Immune |
| **Plague Resistance** | Immune |
| **Bleed Resistance** | Immune |

**Strategies:**

This fight is more of a puzzle than a battle. His eyes always turn red before attacking — hide behind a pillar when they do. Your pathway is blocked with rubble. Three options to clear rubble:
- **Melee:** Any large weapon dual-handed destroys rubble with one R1 hit (even without meeting stat requirements). Large Shield shield bash (L2) also works.
- **Magic:** Hide behind pillar and cast Soul Arrow, Fire Spray, or Flame Toss. ~5–6 Fresh Spice needed.
- **Coax him:** Lure him into attacking the rubble for you. Watch his eye color (yellow = safe, red = hide ASAP).

**Phase 1:** Progress down the right hallway to the upper platform with the glowing ballista. Push the button to shoot him (reduces health by a third). He punches the area, clearing a path downstairs.

#### King Allant (True Form / The Old One)

| Stat | Value |
|------|-------|
| **Stage** | Below the Nexus (End Game) |
| **Demon's Soul** | Beast's Demon's Soul (if evil ending) |
| **Voiced by** | Peter Marinker |

**Description:**
Once you defeat all world bosses, the middle seal in the Nexus opens. You can no longer upgrade your Soul Level by talking to the Maiden in Black, nor will the Monumental offer the Friend's Ring. The Maiden escorts you to the end zone — a beach with ruins, and nearby is the massive Old One. The Maiden beckons the Old One, which invites you to enter its maw-like interior.

Here is the final boss fight against King Allant, who appears as the captive, deformed, and despondent form of the true King Allant. Be sure to pick up **Soulbrandt** after he dies.

**Strategy:**
This "battle" is just a formality. What's left of King Allant XII is very easy to kill, though he has quite a few hit points. He can lunge with the Soulbrandt sword, but he's neither strong nor agile enough to kill you unless you stand still. Imagine a one-on-one fight against a drunken Phosphorescent Slug.

Lock onto King Allant and shoot him with Soul Ray until he dies. Each shot staggers him back, so he squirms in the pool. Otherwise, use any melee weapon, spell, or bare fists. Be sure to pull Soulbrandt from the ground before continuing toward the Maiden in Black.

**Endings:**

| Ending | Action | Reward |
|--------|--------|--------|
| **Good Ending** | Walk away after the Maiden in Black's speech | Maiden in Black's Demon's Soul (60,000 souls or Soulsucker from Yuria) + Trophy. The Old One is put back to sleep. |
| **Evil Ending** | Kill the Maiden in Black | Beast Demon's Soul (200,000 souls) + Red Eye Stone. You succumb to your appetite for souls. |

**Pre-Ending Note:** If you've acquired Demonbrandt, this is your last chance to make Northern Regalia before defeating Flamelurker again in NG+. Requires Shard of Archstone or Nexial Binding to warp back (rear gate closes after Allant fight; walking ends NG). Equipped armor/weapons display with your character in the Pantheon during the next game.
